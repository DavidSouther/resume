CoastBurn (retrograde, full throttle)

Light the engine when your radar altitude reaches a dashed line, then hold retrograde at full throttle. Simulated in 2D around the Mun with real gravity, no drag, and surface velocity ignoring the Mun’s spin.

Simple mode holds mass constant, so thrust acceleration never rises. A real craft gets lighter as it burns, so the final stop will end up above the surface. Advanced mode uses Isp to track mass loss and accounts for this.

Radar altitude is measured from the root part, not from your landing legs. Subtract that offset from every altitude here. Very tall craft can have peculiarities when landing, such as the legs touching down while radar still reads well above zero.
